# The Art of Data Engineering

Data engineering is more than just moving data from point A to point B. It's about crafting reliable, scalable, and maintainable systems that empower organizations to make data-driven decisions.

## Principles of Minimalism in Data

Just like in design, minimalism in data engineering brings clarity.

1.  **Simplicity**: Avoid over-engineering.
2.  **Reliability**: Build for failure.
3.  **Observability**: Know what your system is doing.

> "Data is the new oil."

But without a refinery, it's just sludge.
